Open Habit is a free habit tracker for iPhone and iPad, built around small daily steps and ownership of your data.

Free and open source
Open Habit will be released as open source under the MIT license. Everyone is welcome to contribute, whether through code, ideas, bug reports, documentation, or translations. The source repository is currently private during early development and will become public with the open-source release.

Build routines your way
Create habits with daily completion targets, track progress from interactive Home Screen widgets, and explore your history with calendars and day notes. Use Shortcuts to automate tracking, sync across devices through your private iCloud account, and export or restore your data as JSON.

Tracking works offline, and no Open Habit account is required.
