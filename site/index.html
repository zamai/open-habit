The clear answers

Good to know.

How do I import HabitKit data into Open Habit?

You can move supported HabitKit version 2 JSON exports into Open Habit, a free, open-source habit tracker for iPhone and iPad. Importing into Open Habit is free.

  1. In HabitKit, open Settings → Data Import/Export → Export. Save habitkit_export.json to the Files app.
  2. In Open Habit, open Settings → Import → HabitKit data import, then choose the JSON file.
  3. Review the preview and any conversion warnings. If multiple records exist for the same day, choose how to combine them. Tap Import Data to add the new habits.

Supported habits bring their completion history, notes, categories, and current daily or weekly goals. The standard import preserves existing Open Habit habits and skips matching IDs. Reminders and historical goal changes do not transfer; icons and colors may change. Inverse habits and custom-value tracking are not supported. Keep your original export.

HabitKit is free to download with optional paid Pro upgrades. See its export guide for more help saving your file.

Does Open Habit require an account?

No Open Habit account is required. An Apple Account is needed only for iCloud synchronization.

Where is my data stored?

On your devices and, when iCloud is available, in your private iCloud database. The developer does not receive your Habit data through the app.

Does tracking work offline?

Yes. Changes save locally first and synchronize later when iCloud becomes available.

Is iCloud sync a backup?

It can help restore data after reinstalling, but edits and deletions also synchronize. Export JSON when you want a backup file you control.

Which devices are supported?

Open Habit supports iPhone and iPad on iOS and iPadOS 17 or later, and Apple Watch on watchOS 10 or later with a paired iPhone.

Is there an Android, Mac, or web app?

Not currently. Open Habit is for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.
