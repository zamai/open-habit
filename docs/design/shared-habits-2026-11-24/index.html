Shared Counter Habit — Habit Detail concepts

Scenario: Pull-ups, a Counter Habit (+5 per tap) shared by three Members — You, Taylor, Marta. Today is Tue, Nov 24 2026. Every phone is interactive: switch the period tabs and member chips, and tap days. Shading for each Member is against their own 90th-percentile day (same rule as private Counter Habits), so a 15 from Taylor reads as “a full day” the same way a 50 from Marta does — cooperative, not a leaderboard. Members always keep a fixed order (Owner first), never sorted by score.

Recommendation: A + B combined

The density problem goes away once you stop asking one calendar to answer every question. Split it into two jobs:
  1. “How did we do?” — a Period Summary card at the top (Day / Week / Month / Year tabs, one row per Member with total + bar, group total above). This answers “this week each person did X” in one glance, with zero calendar reading.
  2. “What happened on a given day?” — the Month Calendar stays a calendar. Default to Everyone with split Day Tiles (three tiny bars, concept A); member chips (concept B) collapse it to one person’s familiar single-color calendar with amounts. Tapping a day fills the Day Breakdown card (everyone’s amount + your private Day Note).
  3. Logging rule: calendar taps add +5 only when the You chip is active. In Everyone / other-member modes a tap just selects the day. That removes the ambiguity of “whose day did I just tap?”
Lanes (C) is the best “spreadsheet” for 3 people but grows linearly with Members (up to 10 → 50 rows/month). Stacked bars (D) are lovely for Month/Year and could live inside the summary card later as an alternate visualization.
