The Pull-ups habit is shared by You, Taylor and Marta, and each tap adds 5. Today is Fri, Nov 27 (so you can see a mostly full week). In the phone, tap the Pull-ups card header or its chart to open Habit Detail, and tap the purple button to add 5 to your own count. In Detail, Week is the default view: tap any bar to select that day, and use the chevrons to move between weeks. Use the controls on the right to switch how the selected day is shown and how the Overview card looks. You can also click any card in the gallery.
